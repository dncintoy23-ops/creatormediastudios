Pinterest & YouTube Integrations

How our publishing tools use these platforms • Operated by Creator Media Studios (CreatorMediaLab) & CraftyMyia

What this is

Creator Media Studios runs a private, in-house publishing tool that posts our own original content to our own social accounts. It is an internal studio tool for a small business, not a public app. Visitors and customers never sign in to it, and it never connects to anyone else's Pinterest or Google account.

Pinterest (live)

The CraftyMyia Pinterest Business account shares pins for our own Etsy digital products (SVG cut files and 3D-print STL files), each linking back to the Etsy listing.

YouTube (being set up)

We are creating a Creator Media Studios channel for color-grading walkthroughs, lighting tutorials and Shorts. The YouTube integration will publish only to that channel.

How the sign-in (OAuth 2.0) works

  1. A studio operator clicks Connect Pinterest in our private studio dashboard.
  2. The browser is sent to Pinterest's own consent screen at pinterest.com/oauth, where the operator signs in on Pinterest and reviews the permissions requested.
  3. After the operator approves, Pinterest redirects back to our dashboard with a one-time authorization code and a random state value that we verify.
  4. The dashboard exchanges the code for an access token and a refresh token over HTTPS. Tokens are kept in an access-controlled file on our own computer, are never shown on screen and are refreshed automatically.

We never see or store a Pinterest or Google password.

Permissions we request and why

ScopeWhy we need it
user_accounts:readConfirm which of our own accounts is connected.
boards:readList our own boards so a pin can be assigned to one.
boards:writeCreate a board for a new product category. Pinterest also requires it to create pins.
pins:readCheck that pins we created exist and avoid posting duplicates.
pins:writeCreate pins for our own Etsy products.

For YouTube we will request only the scope needed to upload videos to our own channel, and nothing else.

What we publish

  • Product images and short descriptions for our own Etsy listings, each linking to the listing.
  • Educational photography, lighting and color-grading content (YouTube).
  • No more than a few posts per day, paced by our scheduler. No bulk or spam posting.

Content safety: every pin passes an automatic safety check before it is sent. Content that is adult, suggestive, or otherwise outside Pinterest's Community Guidelines is blocked, and only general-audience product and educational content is published through these integrations.

What we do not do

  • We do not read, collect, store or share data about other Pinterest or YouTube users.
  • We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or build profiles from it.
  • We do not auto-follow, auto-like, auto-comment, scrape or message other users.
  • We do not let third parties connect their accounts to our tool.

Revoking access or deleting data

  • Pinterest: remove our app from the connected apps / permissions section of your Pinterest account settings at any time. When we disconnect an integration, the stored tokens are deleted.
  • Google / YouTube: revoke access at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Questions or deletion requests: digitalcreatorassets@gmail.com.
